🐞 Clashing Warning Not Propagating Post-Optimization

Symptom:
Isomers that were correctly detected as “clashing” didn’t retain the 'clashing' warning in subsequent duplicate filtering steps.

Root Cause:
Inside the loop:

for idx, isomer in enumerate(isomers):
    isomers[idx] = AxialOptModifier(...).modify(...)[0]


the isomer variable still referenced the old (pre-optimized) object.
The warning was assigned to the outdated instance, not the one actually stored in isomers.

Fix:
Re-bound the local variable after replacement:

isomer = isomers[idx]

Now any modifications to isomer.warning persist on the correct object.
